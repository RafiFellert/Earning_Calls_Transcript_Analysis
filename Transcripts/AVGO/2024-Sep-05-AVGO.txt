Summary
Q3 revenue surged 47% year-over-year to $13.1 billion, driven by strong AI and VMware growth. AI revenue is forecast to exceed $12 billion for fiscal 2024, with Q4 guidance raised to $14 billion in revenue and 64% adjusted EBITDA margin. Non-AI semiconductor markets are recovering, and VMware integration is ahead of plan.
