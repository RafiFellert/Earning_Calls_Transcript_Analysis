Fixed income was down 44% compared to last year's exceptional results, but up 11% compared to the second quarter of 2019.
Equity markets was up 13%, driven by record balances in prime, as well as strong performance in cash and equity derivatives, where we matched last year's great results.
Looking forward, while we expect normalization to continue across both investment banking and markets, and most notably in fixed income, the timing and the extent of the normalization is obviously hard to predict.
Wholesale Payments revenue was $1.5 billion, up 5%, driven by higher deposits in fees, largely offset by deposit margin compression.
Security services revenue was $1.1 billion, down 1%, as deposit margin compression was predominantly offset by growth in deposits and fees.
