Teams Premium meets enterprise demand for AI-powered features like intelligent recaps, now generally available.

With Microsoft Viva, we have created a completely new suite for employee experience.
Viva brings together goals, communications, learning, workplace analytics, and employee feedback across industries.
Companies like Dell, Mastercard, and SES are using Viva to help their employees thrive.
Just last week, we announced Copilot for Viva, offering leaders a new way to build high-performance teams while prioritizing both productivity and employee engagement.

Ferrovial, Goldman Sachs, Novo Nordisk, and Rogers all chose E5 to empower their employees with our best-in-class productivity apps along with advanced security, compliance, voice, and analytics.
